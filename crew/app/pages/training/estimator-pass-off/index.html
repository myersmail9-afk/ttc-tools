Training & Pass-Off

Estimator Trainee • Tier 1 • Tier 2 • Tier 3 • PHC Specialty • Complex Removal Specialty

EmployeeTrainer
Start DateReview Date
Purpose: create a clear, repeatable path for TTC Certified Arborists to progress from supervised Estimator Trainee to independent estimating authority through observable behaviors, documented practice, and measurable production results. Advancement is earned by demonstrated competency—not seniority, personality, or sales volume alone.

How to Use This Pass-Off

  • Begin as an Estimator Trainee and complete tiers in order. An Estimator Trainee may participate in and draft estimates, but is not authorized to complete and send estimates without direct oversight and approval from a Tier 3 Qualified Estimator. Specialty endorsements may be pursued after Tier 1 unless the trainer requires Tier 2 first for a particular employee.
  • A checkbox means the employee has demonstrated the standard—not merely received instruction on it. The Tier 3 reviewer remains responsible for approving trainee estimates before they are sent.
  • Use live appointments whenever possible. Training/sample estimates may count where specifically stated.
  • For percentage standards, keep the supporting audit sheet, Jobber report, transcript, or production record with the employee training file.
  • Authority may be narrowed by work type even after a tier is passed. Any estimator must escalate work outside his or her authorized scope.
