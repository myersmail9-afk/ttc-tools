Urban Lumber

Home / Urban Lumber

In the process of removing many trees, we generate copious amounts of logs. If reasonable we salvage the urban wood and have it milled into lumber. We have working relationships with a number of local sawmills and a cabinet shop. We have had great success milling black walnut, black locust, honeylocust, ash, Norway maple, Siberian elm and other species. If you have a special tree that needs to be removed we can make arrangements for transportation and milling of the wood to use in furniture, flooring, or cabinets. Our recycled wood has been used in projects throughout Utah and other states, with pieces in Europe and Asia, with one piece going to an artist who has her piece featured in the Smithsonian.
