Welcome to Total Tree Care

About Us

Total Tree Care has successfully completed thousands of projects over the past 17 years. Notable clients include Utah State University, the LDS Church (meetinghouses, camps, and temples), Utah Department of Transportation, numerous construction companies, cities, HOAs, and hundreds of residential clients. In addition to performing high quality pruning, easy to extremely technical removals, and stump grinding we have been called on to a variety of unusual jobs such as cat rescue, antenna installation, bird house installation, tree house removal, and cutting logs for mushroom culture. There is always something to keep things interesting and challenging.

Other Licenses and Qualifications

These sit outside the arborist path. Each one covers a specific kind of work.

  • Utah Commercial Pesticide Applicator License

    State core exam plus a category exam; required by law to spray pesticides commercially.

    • Trevor Stevens

  • ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualification (TRAQ)

    Two-day course plus a written and field assessment; a risk qualification, not a certification.

    • David Thunell
    • Gabriel Dye

  • TCIA Certified Treecare Safety Professional (CTSP)

    Field safety experience plus a two-day workshop and exam on jobsite safety leadership.

    • David Thunell

Our Team

David Thunell

Operations Manager

David Thunell
ISA Board Certified Master Arborist® UT-4517BT

David, the Operations Manager of Total Tree Care has 14 years of experience in the tree care industry and has spent his entire career with TTC. He is an ISA Board Certified Master Arborist, ISA Certified Tree Climber, ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualified, and TCIA Certified Treecare Safety Professional, and was named Utah Arborist of the Year in 2024. He enjoys climbing and pruning large majestic trees, finding satisfaction in both the technical challenge and the visible transformation of the trees.

David appreciates that every day brings a new puzzle to solve, combining his knowledge of tree biology, soil science, entomology, physics, and mechanics with physical skill and precision. He also enjoys working with and purchasing the specialized gear that makes the job possible.

Outside of work, David enjoys tree climbing, hiking, and backpacking with his wife, Eryn, and their three children. His favorite trees are the White Oak, for its classic beauty and longevity, and the Giant Sequoia, which he admires as a symbol of resilience and growth through adversity.

  • ISA Board Certified Master Arborist
  • ISA Certified Tree Climber
  • ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualified
  • TCIA Certified Treecare Safety Professional

Gabriel Dye

Gabe has worked in the tree care industry for over 12 years and has been with Total Tree Care since March 2020. He holds several certifications, including ISA Certified Arborist, ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualified (TRAQ), and ISA Certified Tree Climber. He enjoys structure pruning and loves returning to client properties to see the trees he’s cared for grow over the years.

Gabe earned his bachelor’s degree in horticulture and developed his passion for trees while working with the campus arborist at BYU–Idaho. He later earned his ISA TRAQ and Certified Arborist qualifications while working at Texas A&M and gained experience in urban forestry with the City of Cheyenne, WY.

Outside of work, Gabe enjoys fishing, reading, gardening, and playing games and sports with his wife, Heather, and their three children. His favorite tree is the Tulip Tree (Liriodendron tulipifera), which he loves for its unique leaves, tulip-shaped flowers, and rarity in Cache Valley.

  • ISA Certified Arborist
  • ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualified (TRAQ)
  • ISA Certified Tree Climber

Tyler Montoya

Tyler Montoya is an ISA Certified Arborist and ISA Certified Tree Climber who loves the thrill, physicality, and camaraderie that come with working at Total Tree Care. He possesses a unique set of skills, having been a soldier in the Army and a firefighter. He is also a licensed EMT and regularly gives training to the crew on first aid and emergency response. He has been with Total Tree Care since March 2025 and brought with him several years of tree work experience.

He is married to Cassidy and has three children. He loves to run marathons, play hockey, and spend time with his kids. His favorite part of tree work is helping clients by removing high-risk trees and branches. His favorite tree is the European Black Pine (also known as the Austrian Pine) due to its aesthetic appeal, especially in winter, and its distinctive branch structure, which makes it enjoyable to climb.

Trevor Stevens

ISA Certified Arborist® WE-10760A

Trevor is one of the most experienced arborists on the Total Tree Care (TTC) crew, having over 13 years of experience in the tree care industry. He has been a Certified Arborist for 11 years and is also a licensed pesticide applicator. He started with TTC in December of 2022. He enjoys the physical nature of the work and spending his days outdoors.

What Trevor finds most rewarding is how every job presents its own set of questions to solve. He takes pride in seeing the visible improvements in each tree he prunes and appreciates how unique the needs of every tree can be. His favorite tree is the Contorted Filbert (Corylus avellana ‘Contorta’), which he admires for its character and form when properly pruned.

Trevor is married to his wife, Cynthia, and they have one child. Outside of work, he enjoys using wood from job sites to create turned pieces such as bowls and pens, and he also spends his free time camping, fishing, and rockhounding.

  • Certified Arborist (11 years)
  • Licensed Pesticide Applicator

Matthew Gil

Matt brings seven years of experience in the tree care industry to the Total Tree Care (TTC) team and has been with TTC since September 2025. He loves the problem-solving and adrenaline high of rigging and crane work, describing it as “a rush to watch hundreds, or even thousands of pounds of tree fly through the air and gracefully land in the desired drop zone.”

Matt enjoys the physical and technical challenges of climbing and working in trees and is currently preparing for his Certified Arborist exam as he continues to build his expertise.

Outside of work, Matt has spent much of his life skateboarding and snowboarding. He also enjoys traveling, riding motorcycles, camping, and being outdoors. Recently, he has taken a strong interest in regenerative agriculture and looks forward to integrating those principles into his arboricultural work. His favorite tree is the Ponderosa Pine (Pinus ponderosa), which he loves for both its appearance and distinctive butterscotch scent.
