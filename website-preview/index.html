Credentials

Certified People on Every Job

ISA Board Certified Master Arborist

David Thunell, our Operations Manager, holds the ISA's highest arborist certification and was named Utah Arborist of the Year in 2024.

5 ISA Certified Arborists

Our crew includes five ISA Certified Arborists who follow the ANSI A300 pruning standard. We never top trees.

Tree Risk Assessment Qualified

Our TRAQ-qualified arborists can tell you whether a tree near your house is a real hazard, and what to do about it.

Safety First

We are bonded and insured. Tyler Montoya is a licensed EMT who trains the crew in first aid, David Thunell is a TCIA Certified Treecare Safety Professional, and Ethan Risenmay is certified in ground and chainsaw operations.

Licensed Pesticide Applicator

Our crew includes a licensed pesticide applicator for plant health care treatments.

Year-Round Work

We work every season. Winter is a good time to prune, because the tree's structure is easy to see.
