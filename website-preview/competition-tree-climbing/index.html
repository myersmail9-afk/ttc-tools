Competition Tree Climbing

Home / Competition Tree Climbing

Total Tree Care has been involved in the Utah Tree Climbing Competition as competitors, judges, technicians, timers, and part of the committee. In that time we have been in the Master's Challenge (finals) 14 times, won the competition 4 times, and won 13 individual events. We look forward to the competition each year because it is an opportunity to learn from other arborists, improve our skills and speed, which carries over on the job day to day, and it is fun to see how we compare with others.

Many of our crew members compete. The skills they sharpen at these events are the same ones they bring to your trees: safe climbing, careful rigging, and quick, calm work aloft.

David Thunell

David, our Operations Manager, is an ISA Board Certified Master Arborist. The Utah Community Forest Council named him Utah Arborist of the Year in 2024.

At the 2025 Utah Tree Climbing Championship, David placed:

  • 1st overall in the preliminary events
  • 1st in Aerial Rescue
  • 2nd in the Master's Challenge (finals)
  • 3rd in the Ascent event

Sources: 2025 UTCC results and Utah Community Forest Council award winners.
