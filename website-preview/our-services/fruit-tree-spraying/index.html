Fruit Tree Spraying

We run an annual fruit tree spray program for homes and orchards throughout Logan and the rest of Cache and Box Elder Counties.

ISA Board Certified Master Arborist on staff · ISA Certified Arborists · Bonded and insured

Signs You May Need This

  • You want a full harvest of clean, usable fruit this year
  • Your apples, pears, cherries, or other fruit trees have had scab, codling moth, or other pest damage in past years
  • You are starting, or restarting, a fruit tree spray program

What's Included

  • An annual program that starts around March and continues through the growing season
  • Insecticides, fungicides, and antibiotics, tailored to what your trees need
  • A foliar (leaf-surface) spray, not a systemic treatment, on fruiting trees. A systemic product can render that season's fruit inedible, so we keep it to the surface
  • Treatments applied by our licensed pesticide applicator, Trevor Stevens
  • The option to switch to a standing annual pre-approval, so you don't have to opt in every year

How It Works

  1. Request a free estimate.
  2. An ISA Certified Arborist visits your property and writes your estimate.
  3. We schedule the work and get it done.

What to Expect on the Day

  • We spray on schedule, starting around March and continuing through the growing season
  • A door hanger is left on the day we come, so you know we've been there even if you're not home
  • By default, you'll get a yearly reminder email to opt back in before we schedule you. Ask to switch to a standing pre-approval and we'll spray automatically every year until you tell us otherwise

Best Time of Year

The spray program starts around March, as trees come out of dormancy, and continues through the growing season. Once pest damage has already happened, spraying will not undo it for that year, so the earlier in the season we start, the better.

About Cost

Every fruit tree spray program starts with a free estimate. Price depends on how many trees you have, their size, and how many visits the program calls for over the season.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does the spray program start?
Around March, as your trees come out of dormancy, and it continues through the growing season.
Is it safe to eat fruit after spraying?
Yes. We use a foliar (surface) spray on fruiting trees, not a systemic product. A systemic treatment can render that season's fruit inedible, so we keep treatment to the leaf surface.
Do I need to sign up every year?
By default, yes. We send a yearly email and you opt back in. If you would rather not think about it, ask us to set you up on a standing pre-approval and we'll schedule you automatically every year until you tell us to stop.
I missed the early spring window. Is it too late?
For that year's fruit, spraying after damage has already happened will not undo it. We will get you started in time for next spring.
Who applies the spray?
Our licensed pesticide applicator, Trevor Stevens.
