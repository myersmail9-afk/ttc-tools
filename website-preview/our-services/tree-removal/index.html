Tree Removal

We remove trees of every size for homes and businesses in Logan and across Cache and Box Elder Counties.

Signs You May Need This

  • The tree is dead, dying, or badly diseased
  • Large cracks, leaning, or broken branches after a storm
  • Roots are lifting your sidewalk, driveway, or foundation
  • The tree crowds your house, power lines, or another tree
  • You are planning construction or a landscape change

What's Included

  • A written estimate before any work begins
  • Full cleanup. Any branch we cut that hits the ground gets hauled away
  • Trunk wood hauled off, unless you ask us to leave firewood
  • A low stump cut close to the ground, with stump grinding available as a separate, optional add-on
  • Crane removals for large or hard-to-access trees
  • Brush removal and hauling
  • Response for storm-damaged or hanging branches when we have someone available

How It Works

  1. Request a free estimate.
  2. An ISA Certified Arborist visits your property and writes your estimate.
  3. We schedule the work and get it done.

What to Expect on the Day

  • We ask you to clear the drop zone of anything movable before we start
  • If we need to drive on your lawn, we lay down industrial ground mats to prevent ruts
  • Full cleanup when the work is done

Best Time of Year

Tree removal does not follow a season the way pruning does. It is safe to schedule any time of year, whenever it is convenient for you.

About Cost

Every tree removal starts with a free estimate. What drives the price is the tree's size, how hard it is to access, whether a crane is needed, and whether you want the stump ground out. We will walk you through all of it before any work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean up all the branches and wood?
Yes. Full cleanup is included in every price. We haul away everything that hits the ground unless you ask us to leave firewood.
Will you remove the stump too?
We can. Every removal includes a low stump cut close to the ground. Stump grinding is a separate, optional line item if you want the stump gone completely.
What if a branch is hanging over my house right now?
Call us. If we have someone available, we can often put the immediate hazard on the ground the same visit, then follow up with a full quote for the rest of the work.
Is there a best time of year to remove a tree?
No. Unlike pruning, removal does not follow a season. Schedule it whenever works best for you.
Can you remove a tree with a crane if my yard is tight?
Often, yes. A crane lifts each cut piece and sets it down wherever it can reach, so a tight yard or obstacles under the tree usually are not a problem.
