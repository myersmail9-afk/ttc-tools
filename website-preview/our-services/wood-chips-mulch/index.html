How It Works

  1. Already having tree work done? Ask us to leave that job's own chips instead of hauling them away. No sign-up needed.
  2. Want a delivery on its own? Sign up online. Enter your email and we'll send you a 6-digit code, no password needed.
  3. Enter your address, then drag the pin to your exact drop spot on the map and add a photo of the spot.
  4. Choose how many loads you want and pick a tier: a free option, or a paid tier that moves you up the list. Answer whether a truck pulling a chipper trailer can reach your spot.
  5. The office checks your details, usually within a couple of business days. Once you're active, the crew drops chips at your spot when it's your turn.
  6. Come back to the sign-up page anytime to update your info, change your tier, pause, or leave the list.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the wood chips really free?
Leaving chips from your own job is free. On the sign-up list, there's a free option, plus paid tiers that move you up the list faster. You're only billed after each drop, and you'll see the exact price before you choose a tier.
How do I sign up for a delivery?
Sign up on our wood chips page. You'll sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your email, no password needed, then give us your address, drag a pin to your exact drop spot, and add a photo of the spot.
How long will it take?
There's no set schedule. Free drops happen when a nearby job leaves us with extra chips, so most people on the free list wait anywhere from a few weeks to a couple of months between drops. A paid tier can move you up the list faster.
How much would I get from my own job?
A typical removal-and-pruning job produces roughly three-quarters of a load, about 8 to 10 cubic yards. A full truckload is about 12 to 15 yards.
Can I get chips without having tree work done?
Yes. Sign up on our wood chips page, choose how many loads you want, and pick a free option or a paid tier. There's no guaranteed delivery date on the free tier.
Can my neighbor get chips too?
If a neighbor within about a mile wants a load, we can drop it there instead of yours.
Are these the same as the bagged mulch from the store?
No, and that's a good thing. Our chips are unscreened, straight from the chipper: a mix of leaves, twigs, wood, and bark. They break down and feed the soil better than manicured, bagged mulch.
How should I spread them around my trees?
Lay them 2 to 4 inches deep around the base, but leave a ring of bare dirt right at the trunk. Piling chips against the trunk (a "mulch volcano") traps moisture against the bark and can kill the tree.
Can I change my mind after I sign up?
Yes. Come back to the sign-up page anytime and sign in with your email. You can update your address or photo, change your tier, pause your drops, or leave the list.
