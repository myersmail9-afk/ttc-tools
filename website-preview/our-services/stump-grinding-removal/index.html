Stump Grinding & Removal

Stump grinding is an effective way to remove stumps and make way for landscaping, a new tree, or to improve appearance. We own one of the largest, most powerful stump grinders in the area. This allows us to grind deeper and faster than other machines. In fact we been called in to finish grinding many stumps started by other companies or homeowners.

We offer the option of leaving the stump chips which can be composted or used in landscaping and gardening or we take them away, leveling the ground leaving little evidence that a stump was removed. Heavy equipment is often used to remove large stumps which can be expensive, have a large impact on the landscape, with the added task of loading the stump onto a truck, necessitating a dump fee. Our machine can turn the largest of stumps into wood chips that can be left on site or removed with a minimal of equipment. Please contact us for stump grinding prices and options.
