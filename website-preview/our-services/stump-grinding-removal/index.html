Stump Grinding & Removal

We grind and remove stumps for properties throughout Logan and all of Cache and Box Elder Counties.

Signs You May Need This

  • An old stump is in the way of new landscaping or a new tree
  • A stump is a tripping hazard in the yard
  • You want a finished, mowable lawn again
  • Another company left a stump unfinished

What's Included

  • A written estimate before any work begins
  • We file the Blue Stakes utility locate ticket in our own name and complete it before we grind
  • Two grinder sizes: a small grinder that fits about a 40-inch gate but only grinds just below the surface, and a larger grinder that grinds all the way down but needs more room
  • Your choice: leave the stump chips on site, or have us haul them away as a separate line item

How It Works

  1. Request a free estimate.
  2. An ISA Certified Arborist visits your property and writes your estimate.
  3. We schedule the work and get it done.

What to Expect on the Day

  • We grind the stump plus roots within about a one-to-two-foot radius. We do not chase roots further out because it damages the yard, and the roots left behind break down on their own over time
  • Blue Stakes covers utility-owned lines only. Sprinkler lines and other private lines are yours to mark before we arrive
  • If you want a new tree in the same spot, tell us. We can grind deeper to make room for the new roots to establish

Best Time of Year

Stump grinding, like tree removal, is not tied to a season. Schedule it whenever works for you.

About Cost

Every stump job starts with a free estimate. Stump grinding is priced and quoted separately from the rest of a job, not folded into the general cleanup price. What drives the cost is the stump's size, how many stumps, and how easy the area is to access.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will grinding the stump also remove the roots?
Not completely. Grinding removes the stump plus roots within about a one-to-two-foot radius. We don't chase roots further because it tears up the yard; leftover roots break down on their own over time.
Do I need to mark my sprinkler lines?
Yes. We file the Blue Stakes locate for utility lines, but Blue Stakes does not mark privately owned lines like sprinklers or drip systems. Please mark those yourself before we arrive.
Can you grind a stump in a tight side yard?
Often, yes. Our smaller grinder fits through about a 40-inch gate, though it only grinds just below the surface. A full, deep grind needs the larger machine and more room.
What happens to the wood chips from grinding?
Your choice. We can leave them on site for you to use, or haul them away as a separate line item.
Can I plant a new tree where the stump was?
Yes. Let us know ahead of time and we will grind deeper so the new tree has room to establish.
