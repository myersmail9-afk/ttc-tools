About Cost

Every consultation starts with a free estimate to scope the visit. A formal written risk assessment report is a separate, paid service; a free email summary of the walkthrough is included. What drives the price is how many trees are involved and whether you need the formal report.

Frequently Asked Questions

What level of risk assessment do you do?
The International Society of Arboriculture defines three levels. Most of our visits are a Level 2: a close, full 360-degree walk-around, ground-based and visual, without special tools. A Level 3 assessment brings in tools like core sampling, height measurement, or a climb or drone inspection for a defect that needs a closer look.
Is there such a thing as a "no risk" tree?
No. As long as a tree is standing it carries some level of risk. Removal is the only way to eliminate that risk; everything else, including pruning and cabling, mitigates it.
Do I need a formal written report?
Not always. We can send a free email summary of the walkthrough for most situations. If you need documentation, for example for an insurance claim or a legal matter, we also offer a paid, formal written tree risk assessment.
Who does the consulting?
David Thunell, our ISA Board Certified Master Arborist, and our other ISA Certified Arborists.
