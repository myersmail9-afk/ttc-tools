Signs You May Need This

  • Pale yellow leaves with darker green veins
  • A canopy that looks sparse or thin, or branches dying back
  • Insects, sticky residue, or a powdery white coating on the leaves
  • A tree that just is not thriving, and you don't know why

What's Included

  • Diagnosis before treatment. We prefer a soil sample sent to the Utah State Analytics Lab over guessing, because trees need a different nutrient balance than turf, and too much of one nutrient can block the tree from taking up the others
  • Soil and trunk-injected treatments for many nutrient and pest problems, such as iron and manganese chlorosis, aphids, and several common scale and borer insects
  • Foliar (leaf) spraying for fungus and insect problems, like powdery mildew or aphids
  • A plant growth regulator instead of a spray for some conditions, like powdery mildew, when it offers broader benefit to the tree
  • Treatments applied by our licensed pesticide applicator, Trevor Stevens
  • A door hanger left at your home on every spray visit, so you know we were there

How It Works

  1. Request a free estimate.

What to Expect on the Day

  • We diagnose before we treat. A nutrient deficiency almost always shows in the leaves, so we start there and follow up with a soil sample when needed
  • A door hanger left behind so you know we came, even if you were not home

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you spray, or is everything injected into the soil?
Both. Many nutrient and pest problems are treated with a soil or trunk-injected product. For fungus and insect problems, like powdery mildew or aphids, we also spray the leaves directly.
How do you know what's wrong with my tree?
We start by reading the leaves. A nutrient deficiency almost always shows there. For an accurate answer, we prefer a soil sample sent to the Utah State Analytics Lab rather than guessing or using a blanket fertilizer.
Who applies the treatment?
Our licensed pesticide applicator, Trevor Stevens.
Will I know when you've been here?
Yes. We leave a door hanger at your home on every spray visit.
I have a fruit tree. Is that a different service?
Yes. Fruit trees on a spray program get their own annual plan. See our Fruit Tree Spraying page.
